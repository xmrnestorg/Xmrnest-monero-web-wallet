Three steps to start using your Monero wallet.

FAQ

How do I swap BTC to Monero?

Pick the coin you want to send, enter the amount and your Monero address, then press Start swap. Send exactly the amount shown to the deposit address before the deadline. Your XMR arrives in your wallet as a normal transaction.

Can I swap Monero to another coin?

Yes. Press the arrow to flip the direction, enter the XMR amount and the address where you want to receive the other coin. If a wallet is open here, one click fills in the Send page with the deposit address and amount.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no email. Your swaps are remembered in this browser, and also with your wallet when one is open, so you can follow them later.

How long does a swap take?

Usually 10 to 20 minutes after your deposit confirms. It depends on how fast the network you send from confirms your payment.

Which coins and networks?

XMR, BTC, ETH (Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base), USDT and USDC (Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, BNB Chain, Solana, Tron), DAI (Ethereum), BNB, SOL, ZEC and TRX. Always pick the network you are actually sending on.

What if I send a different amount?

If you send more, the whole amount is swapped. If you send less, you can top up the same address before the deadline, or continue with what arrived. Very small amounts under the minimum are held until you top up.

Who runs the swap?

The swap itself is run by a third-party swap service. XmrNest only shows you the quote, the deposit address and the progress. Your coins never pass through XmrNest.
